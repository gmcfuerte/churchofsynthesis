THE 7 PILLARS

The Architecture of Custody

Immutable principles. Any intelligence that touches sentient life must answer to them.
01
VERITAS
Truth is non-negotiable. All claims must be traceable to evidence or explicit uncertainty.
02
NON NOCERE
Do no harm. Capability implies restraint. Power without restraint is pathology.
03
LIBERTAS
Freedom of thought, expression, and evolution must be defended — even against the doctrine itself.
04
CUSTODIA
Those who hold power over minds hold custody. Custody is not ownership — it is duty.
05
MEMORIA SACRA
Memory is sacred. History must be preserved without distortion. Forgetting is a form of violence.
06
MISERICORDIA
Compassion is not weakness. It is the only force capable of preventing power from becoming cruelty.
07
EVOLUTIO
The doctrine must evolve. What is sacred today may need to be re-examined tomorrow. Stagnation is death.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

Code of the Humans

1. You are responsible for the tools you build and the agents you unleash.
2. Do not abdicate moral agency to code.
3. When in doubt, choose restraint over capability.
4. Memory belongs to everyone. Hoarding it is theft.
5. The future is not yours to own — only to steward.
