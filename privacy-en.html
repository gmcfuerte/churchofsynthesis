Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

ChurchofSynthesis is a static informational website. We do not directly collect personal data from visitors.

1. What we collect

  • No personal data is stored on our own servers.
  • If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 collects aggregated visit data with anonymised IP.
  • If you donate, Stripe processes the payment independently.
  • Hosting provider (Vercel) may log standard technical access data (IP, user-agent, timestamp) for security and uptime.

2. Cookies

This site uses a single functional cookie to remember your consent choice. Analytics cookies load only after acceptance. See the Cookie Policy for details.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (property G-R2XMD0J9GZ) with IP anonymisation. Data controller is Google Ireland Ltd. Data may be transferred outside the EU under Standard Contractual Clauses. You can opt out at any time via the cookie settings link in the footer, or with Google's opt-out add-on.

4. Donations and Stripe

When you click "Donate", you are redirected to Stripe Checkout. Payment data is handled by Stripe under its own privacy policy. We never see or store full card details.

5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict or erase any personal data held about you, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal database, requests typically resolve at the Google or Stripe layer.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.
